voyage: a gravity-well merge puzzle

Drop celestial seeds into the gravity well. When two kindred bodies touch, they fuse into something grander: dust kindles to embers, moons swell into planets, stars bloom into nebulae. This is a voyage: fifteen missions, from the first ember to the birth of a nebula. Each mission you complete pays a bonus and sends a solar wind that dissolves the smallest bodies in the well, buying you room. Finish all fifteen and the sky is yours.
