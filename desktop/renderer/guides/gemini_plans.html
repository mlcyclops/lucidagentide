1Do this in LUCID right now

The short version

Paste a free AI Studio API key into the Gemini card today (zero dollars, full model catalog, rate-limited). If you want a subscription sign-in on your Workspace account, buy Gemini Code Assist Standard (about $19 per user per month, annual) and fill the GCP project ID field. Do not buy Google AI Pro expecting the OAuth button to work, and do not buy Gemini Enterprise for IDE access at all.

Path A: free, working in five minutes (recommended start)

Get an API key at aistudio.google.com/apikey with any Google account. In LUCID: Settings > Providers > Google · Gemini, paste it into the GEMINI_API_KEY box and Save. The full commercial Gemini catalog appears in the model picker. The free tier has daily rate limits; add billing to the same key later and it becomes pay-as-you-go with no subscription.

Path B: subscription OAuth that still works in 2026

Since June 18, 2026 the Connect via OAuth button (the Gemini CLI / Code Assist flow) only accepts accounts holding Gemini Code Assist Standard or Enterprise licenses, or the Workspace AI Ultra Access add-on. To use it: buy Code Assist Standard for your Workspace user in Google Cloud, enable the Gemini for Google Cloud API on a project, put that project's ID in the GCP project ID field on the Gemini card, Save, then Connect via OAuth with your Workspace account.

Why your sign-in "succeeded" but nothing appeared

Google's consent page says Authentication Successful before it checks your entitlement. The rejection happens one step later, during Code Assist onboarding. LUCID now surfaces that rejection as a red toast and a note on the Gemini card, so a failed connect tells you which of these paths you are missing.

Path C: pay-per-token under your own GCP governance

The Google Cloud · Gemini Enterprise card (the backend formerly called Vertex AI) needs no subscription at all: run gcloud auth application-default login, set project + location, and you get every Gemini model billed per token against your GCP project. Best when you already live in Google Cloud and want invoices, quotas, and IAM instead of a consumer plan.

3How the pricing got this confusing

Google has restructured Gemini's packaging roughly every six months. The pattern worth internalizing: generous free launch, consumer tier shuffle, then consolidation behind paid or enterprise SKUs.

Feb 2024

Bard becomes Gemini. Workspace gets paid add-ons: Gemini Business ($20/seat) and Gemini Enterprise ($30/seat). Consumers get Google One AI Premium at $19.99.

Jan 2025

Add-ons discontinued. Gemini features fold INTO Workspace Business/Enterprise plans with a roughly $2/seat price bump. Good deal if you wanted the sidebar; irrelevant for IDE use.

Jun 2025

Gemini CLI launches with a famously generous free tier (about 1,000 requests/day on a personal Gmail). This is the tier LUCID's OAuth button was built on.

2025

Google One AI Premium is rebranded Google AI Pro; AI Ultra appears at $249.99, later restructured. A $4.99 AI Plus tier arrives for light users.

Oct 2025

Gemini Enterprise launches as the org agent platform (about $21/seat Business, $30/seat Enterprise), replacing the Agentspace branding.

Nov 2025

Antigravity IDE ships free in public preview with multi-model access. Honeymoon quotas: about 250 free requests/day.

Mar 2026

Antigravity quota crunch: free tier cut to about 20 requests/day, refresh cycles move from 5 hours to weekly. Hitting a cap can lock a tier for up to 7 days.

Jun 18, 2026

The big cutoff. Gemini CLI and the Code Assist IDE extensions stop serving the free individual tier AND Google AI Pro/Ultra subscribers. Login with Google is removed for consumer accounts. Only Code Assist Standard/Enterprise seats and Workspace AI Ultra Access continue. Google's stated migration path for consumers is Antigravity.

The lesson for a small business

Do not build a workflow on a Google free tier's continued existence. Anchor on the two things Google has never clawed back: the pay-as-you-go API (AI Studio / Vertex) and per-seat developer licenses with contracts behind them (Code Assist).

6When Gemini Enterprise earns its seats

Gemini Enterprise (about $30/seat/month, Business tier around $21) is a different product category: a governed agent workspace for a whole company. It makes sense when most of these are true:

  • You are rolling out agents to non-developers (ops, contracts, logistics analysts) who need a chat/agent UI, not an IDE.
  • You need central governance: audit trails, data-source connectors with permissions, and admin-controlled model access across the org.
  • You want agents grounded in company data (Drive, SharePoint, Salesforce connectors) with access control enforced by the platform rather than by you.
  • Seat math beats usage math: heavy daily use by 10+ people can make flat seats cheaper than per-token.

For your situation

A solo or few-person shop doing development work gets nothing from Gemini Enterprise that an AI Studio key, a Code Assist seat, or Vertex pay-as-you-go does not already provide cheaper. Revisit it the day you have a team of non-developer users who need governed agents over shared company data. For defense or CUI-adjacent work, note that your existing gov-gateway route (AskSage) already covers the compliance-governed path in LUCID.
