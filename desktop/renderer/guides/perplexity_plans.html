1Do this in LUCID right now

The short version

Perplexity in LUCID is API key only. Get a key from your Perplexity account (API settings), paste it into the PERPLEXITY_API_KEY box on the Perplexity card in Settings > Providers, Save, and the Sonar models appear in the picker. There is no Connect button to look for; that is by design, not an omission.

Why there is no OAuth button on this card

Perplexity's sign-in is an interactive flow: it emails you a one-time passcode and expects a human to type it into a live page. LUCID's OAuth broker automates standard redirect-and-token flows; it cannot sit in your inbox and forward an OTP. Rather than show a button that would always fail, the card hides it and asks for the API key directly.

Agentic Pro Search is metered per query, not per token

Perplexity's agentic Pro Search runs at roughly $14 to $22 per 1,000 queries on top of token costs, depending on configuration. Budget for it separately if your workflows fire many searches.

4What Perplexity is good for (and not)

Good for: web-grounded answers with citations. Sonar models search the live web and return sourced answers, which makes them the right pick in LUCID for research questions, current-events lookups, fact-checking with references, and competitive or market scans where "says who?" matters.

Not for: coding. Perplexity is not a general coding model family; do not route agentic coding work at it. Keep a coding provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, Copilot, or similar) as your main lane and treat Perplexity as the research sidearm.

A useful pairing

A common LUCID setup: a coding model as the default, with a Sonar model selected just for research tasks. At $0.20 to $5 per 1M tokens the grounded lookups cost pennies, and each answer arrives with citations you can verify.
