Behavior notes
  • The Agent box is the real metric .tile (copied verbatim) at the rail's measured width: 76 x 49 px. The art takes the number's slot (28 px), the name takes the label slot and ellipsizes.
  • The Lucid is the mascot itself: the 20 x 26 BASE_20 grid and its meditate rows from mascot.ts at 1 css px per cell. Typing is three new arm diffs plus a keyboard row, authored the mascot way (rows() diffs).
  • Block = a pixel Brick Brigade brick: 1 px outline, lit top-left and shaded bottom-right bevel, 3 px material sides with a neon seam and rivets, a dark recessed window, a pixel glass streak.
  • Idle: meditating, 1 px head bob, stepped aura, slow scarf flutter. Working: typing bursts with a rest, key pixels flash, faster scarf. Glance: about every 60 s; ml-notebooks glances every 6.5 s here for review.
  • Every animation is a frame swap (steps), never a tween, so the art stays on whole pixels at 1x, 2x and 4x.
  • Hover: 140 ms intent, 260 ms grace. The module prefers the left of the box, clamps inside the window, its nub tracks the box. Reduced motion stops every loop; pose and LED still read.
